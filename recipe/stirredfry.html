Vegetable Stir Fry

Description

A quick and healthy vegetable stir fry packed with nutrients and flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 cup sliced bell peppers
  • 1 cup sliced carrots
  • 1 cup snap peas
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.
  2. Add minced garlic and sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add broccoli, bell peppers, carrots, and snap peas to the skillet. Stir-fry for 5-7 minutes until vegetables are tender-crisp.
  4. Drizzle soy sauce over the vegetables and toss to coat evenly.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot over rice or noodles.
