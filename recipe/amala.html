Amala

Description

A traditional Nigerian dish made from yam flour and water, often served with ewedu and gbegiri soup.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups yam flour
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, mix the yam flour and salt.
  2. Gradually add water while stirring to form a smooth dough.
  3. Knead the dough until it is smooth and elastic.
  4. Shape the dough into small balls and flatten them into discs.
  5. Cook the amala in boiling water until it is firm and no longer sticky.
  6. Serve hot with your favorite soup.
