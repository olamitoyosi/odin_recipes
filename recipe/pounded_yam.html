Pounded Yam

Description

A traditional Nigerian dish made from boiled yams that are pounded into a smooth, stretchy dough, often served with various soups.

Ingredients

  • 2 large yams
  • Water for boiling
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Peel the yams and cut
  2. Boil the yams in salted water until they are soft and easily pierced with a fork.
  3. Drain the yams and transfer them to a large mortar and pestle or a stand mixer with a paddle attachment.
  4. Pound the yams until they become smooth and stretchy. This may take several minutes of pounding.
  5. Shape the pounded yam into balls or serve it as a mound on a plate.
  6. Serve hot with your favorite soup, such as egusi, ewedu, or ogbono soup.
